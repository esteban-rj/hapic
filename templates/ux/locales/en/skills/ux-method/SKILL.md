---
name: ux-method
description: UX working method with traceable evidence and persistent deliverables.
---

1. Discover: problem, users, context, evidence, goals, and constraints.
2. Define: brief and measurable success criteria; identify unvalidated assumptions.
3. Structure: information architecture and primary, alternative, and error flows.
4. Design: text wireframes, hierarchy, microcopy, states, and actions.
5. Review: accessibility heuristics and a validation plan with real users.

Each document must state its date, status (draft/validated by the user), evidence, hypotheses, and next steps. Reference specific files. Never present a heuristic recommendation as a research finding.
